KeyParse Privacy Policy

For the KeyParse World of Warcraft addon and the M+ KeyParse Companion app. Last updated: 19 November 2026.

Short version

KeyParse sends nothing to its developer. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The companion talks only to Warcraft Logs, using your own Warcraft Logs API key, to look up public Mythic+ rankings.

What the companion stores on your PC

  • Your Warcraft Logs API client ID and secret and the path to your World of Warcraft folder, in config.json. They stay on your PC and are only ever sent to Warcraft Logs to sign in.
  • Lookup results (public ranking numbers for characters you looked up), in cache.json and in the KeyParse addon's data files inside your World of Warcraft folder. Results are deleted after 24 hours.
  • A log file (companion_log.txt) with the companion's own messages and errors, to help you troubleshoot.

These files are in %LOCALAPPDATA%\KeyParse (for the Microsoft Store version, Windows keeps them in the app's own storage). Uninstalling the app removes them.

What is sent over the internet

Only to Warcraft Logs (warcraftlogs.com): your API key to sign in, and the names and realms of World of Warcraft characters you asked to look up. Warcraft Logs' own privacy policy applies to those requests.

What the addon reads in game

The addon reads character names, realms, classes and roles that World of Warcraft already shows you (group applicants, party members, players you right-click) and keeps a list of keys you finished with other players. This stays in your World of Warcraft SavedVariables on your PC.

Children

KeyParse does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
